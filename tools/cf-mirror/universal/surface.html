Touchpad controls
One finger swipe: move remote cursor, wherever it already is.
Tap: left click at remote cursor. Double tap: double click. Hold ~0.6 s: right click.
Fast finger flick: pointer glides briefly with inertia. Drag button: toggle left-button hold for dragging.
Two fingers: pinch to zoom; pan the magnified image. At 1×, two-finger slide scrolls the remote page.
Fit/Fill changes viewing zoom only; remote window coordinates stay calibrated. ABC opens the phone's normal keyboard; Keys sends missing desktop keys.
