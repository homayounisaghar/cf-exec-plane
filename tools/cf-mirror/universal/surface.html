Touchpad controls
One finger swipe: move remote cursor, wherever it already is.
Tap: left click at remote cursor. Double tap: double click. Hold ~0.6 s: right click.
Finger movement is direct without momentum. Tap ⌖ to toggle an instant predictive touchpad circle; the real remote cursor remains in the streamed image. Drag holds left button.
Two fingers: pinch to zoom; pan the magnified image. At 1×, two-finger slide scrolls the remote page.
Fit/Fill changes viewing zoom only.
